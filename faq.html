Common questions

If your question is not here, call or WhatsApp the office and ask directly.

Where is Bagu General Enterprises located?
Head office is at Rukidi III Street, P.O. Box 253, Fort Portal, Uganda. It is a walk-in office: contractors and households can come in, discuss the job and get a quotation.
What does the company actually supply?
Six lines: hardware and building materials, transport, construction, well drilling, machine hire, and general supplies. The services page on the home site lists each one.
Do you issue receipts?
Yes. Every sale is backed by an official numbered receipt. If you are buying for an organisation, tell the counter staff so your name or department appears on it.
How do I get a quotation?
Call or WhatsApp the office with the items or the work, the quantity and the site location. For construction and drilling we normally visit the site before quoting, because ground conditions change the price.
Can I hire a machine without an operator?
Machine hire is arranged with the office. The operator and fuel arrangement are agreed before the machine leaves the yard, and that is written on the hire document.
Do you deliver materials?
Transport is one of our own service lines, so delivery is quoted together with the materials once we know the distance and access to the site.
Do you work outside Fort Portal?
Yes, across the region around Fort Portal. Distance affects transport cost, so tell us the exact site location when requesting a quotation.
How can a supplier or contractor verify the company?
The documents page explains exactly which papers we issue for supplier verification and how to request them. We do not publish certificates on this website.
Are you hiring?
Applications are welcome at any time across hardware, driving, site work, stores and office roles. The careers page explains how to apply. We do not charge anyone to apply.
Who built this website?
DeryCode Technologies, a software company based in Kyenjojo, Uganda.
