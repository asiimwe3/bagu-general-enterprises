Privacy Notice

Plain language about what we collect, why, and who sees it.

This notice explains what happens to information you give Bagu General Enterprises Ltd through this website, by phone, by WhatsApp or at the office. It is written plainly rather than in legal language.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, phone number, email, site location and the details of what you want quoted. If you apply for a job, the CV and referees you choose to send.
  • Nothing hidden. This website does not run advertising trackers, does not build a profile of you, and does not sell data to anyone.
  • Website basics. The site stores a small offline cache in your browser so it opens on a weak connection. It holds page files, not your personal information.

Why we hold it

  • To prepare a quotation and supply what you ordered.
  • To issue your official numbered receipt and keep the sales record the law requires.
  • To contact you about a delivery, a site visit or a job you applied for.

Who sees it

Only the staff who need it to serve you: counter staff, accounts, and the crew assigned to your job. We do not publish customer names, phone numbers or site details. We do not pass your details to other companies for marketing.

We share information with a third party only where you ask us to, or where the law requires it, for example a tax record or a court order.

How long we keep it

Sales and receipt records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Job applications are kept on file while they may still be considered, and you can ask us to remove yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Message baguent@yahoo.com or call 0772 433687.

Photographs on this website

Some photographs on this site are licensed images that illustrate the type of machine or work described. They are credited on the photo credits page. Photographs of customer sites or staff are only published with permission.
