Careers

Work With Us

We hire across hardware, transport, construction, stores and office roles. Applications are free and always welcome.

How we hire

We Keep Applications on File year round

Bagu General Enterprises does not always advertise vacancies, but we hire steadily as the hardware store, the transport fleet and the construction crews grow. If your trade appears below, send your details and we will contact you when a position fits.

Retail

Hardware & sales

Counter sales, stock control, loading and store support. Comfortable with figures, honest with customers, able to lift and count stock accurately.

Fleet

Drivers & operators

Tipper and truck drivers, backhoe and excavator operators, roller operators. A valid Ugandan driving licence and a clean record are required for every driving role.

Site

Construction crew

Masons, steel fixers, site supervisors and general labour. Site supervisors should be able to read drawings and keep a daily record of materials and labour.

Stores

Inventory & dispatch

Receiving deliveries, verifying quantities against delivery notes, recording every issue with a numbered document.

Office

Accounts & administration

Receipting, reconciling cash and mobile money, filing, and following up customer accounts. Accuracy matters more than speed here.

Field

Drilling & water

Drilling assistants and pump technicians for well drilling work. Willingness to travel to site and stay until the job is finished.

How to apply

Three steps, no forms to post, no application fee. Bagu General Enterprises never charges anyone to apply for a job.

  1. Send your details

    WhatsApp or email your name, the role you want, where you live and your phone number. Attach a CV if you have one. A written CV is not compulsory.

  2. We call shortlisted applicants

    If your trade matches a current need, we call you to talk about the work, the site and the pay for that role.

  3. Bring your documents

    Before starting: national ID, a referee we can call, and for driving roles a valid driving licence. We check papers before a trial day, not after.

No vacancies advertised today. Nothing is currently open for a specific post, and we will not pretend otherwise. Applications sent now are held and reviewed when a role opens.

What we look for

Honesty with stock and cash

Every item that leaves the yard is receipted. We value people who record things properly even when nobody is watching.

Punctuality

Sites open early and customers arrive early. Turning up on time matters more than working fast.

Care for the machines

Operators who check oil, water and tyres and report faults before they become breakdowns.

Respect for customers

A builder on a tight budget deserves the same courtesy as a large contractor.
