Terms of Service

The basis on which we supply hardware, transport, construction, drilling and machine hire.

These are the terms on which Bagu General Enterprises Ltd supplies hardware, transport, construction work, drilling, machine hire and general supplies. They do not remove any right you have under Ugandan law.

Quotations

  • A quotation is valid for the period written on it. Prices for cement, steel and fuel move, so an expired quotation may be reissued at current prices.
  • For construction, drilling and machine hire, the quotation follows a site visit. Ground conditions, access and distance are priced after we have seen the site.
  • Work outside the quotation is agreed in writing before it is done, not added afterwards.

Orders and payment

  • Every sale is receipted with an official numbered receipt. Ask for it; it is your proof of purchase and of the amount paid.
  • Materials are released or delivered against payment unless a written credit arrangement has been agreed by the company.
  • Bank account details are issued on our invoice letterhead only. Treat any account number sent by chat or text with suspicion and confirm it by calling the office.

Delivery and transport

  • Delivery dates are given in good faith. Rain, road condition and vehicle breakdowns can delay a trip, and we tell you as soon as we know.
  • Offloading is normally the buyer's arrangement unless the quotation says otherwise.
  • Materials should be checked and counted at delivery. Report shortages or breakages at that moment so they can be resolved while the vehicle is still there.

Machine hire

  • Hire is charged by the hour, day or agreed task, as written on the hire document.
  • The operator and fuel arrangement are agreed before the machine leaves the yard.
  • The hirer provides safe access to the site. Damage caused by an unsafe site or by work the machine was not hired to do is charged to the hirer.

Returns and faults

  • Cement, aggregates and cut materials cannot be returned once they have left the yard, because their condition can no longer be verified.
  • Hardware items returned unused, undamaged and in their packaging may be exchanged within seven days, with the receipt.
  • If something we supplied is faulty, tell the office with the receipt number and we will inspect it.

Liability

We are responsible for supplying what was ordered and for doing agreed work with reasonable skill and care. We are not responsible for losses caused by a site we were not told about, by instructions given on site, or by events outside our control.

Careers

Applications are free. Bagu General Enterprises never asks anyone for money to be considered for a job, and any message asking for a fee in our name is fraudulent. Report it to the office.
